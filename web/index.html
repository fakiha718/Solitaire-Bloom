🌸 💖 Princess Solitaire - Cute Guide 🎀 ✨

✨ Objective

Build all 4 Foundation piles up from Ace to King by matching suit: 💖 Hearts, 💎 Diamonds, 🌸 Clubs, and 👑 Spades!

🎀 Rules & Princess Strategy

  • Tableau Stacking: Stack cards in descending rank with alternating colors (e.g. Sweet Rose 7 on Royal Plum 8).
  • Empty Columns: Only a King 👑 (or a royal run starting with a King) can occupy an empty tableau slot.
  • Stock & Waste: Draw from the Stock Queue to the Waste Stack. When the Stock is empty, click to recycle.
  • Multi-Card Runs: Drag any face-up card in a column to move it and all cards underneath it in one smooth movement!
  • Quick Moves: Double-click any playable card to instantly send it to the best available foundation or tableau pile.

🎓 DSA Academic Architecture

Engineered from scratch using pure pointer-based Queue (Stock), Stack (Waste & Foundations), and DoublyLinkedList (Tableaux) supporting instant $O(1)$ sublist splicing!
